Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Old Apostolic Church New Zealand ("OACNZ", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the OACNZ mobile application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who We Are

The OACNZ App is operated by the Old Apostolic Church New Zealand for the purpose of managing church membership, attendance, reporting, and financial records within our church community. The App is intended exclusively for authorised church members and administrators.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Full name, date of birth, gender, contact details (email address, phone number, physical address).
  • Church Membership Information: Membership status, priestship/eldership/overseer assignments, attendance records, and Sunday School participation.
  • Financial Records: Contribution records, asset register entries, and cashbook data associated with your local church unit.
  • Device Information: Device token used to deliver push notifications to your device via Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Usage Data: App activity logs for diagnostic and security purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage and maintain church membership records.
  • To provide role-based access to reports and administrative functions within the church hierarchy.
  • To send push notifications related to your church activities, approvals, and updates.
  • To generate reports for church leadership (Priests, Elders, Overseers).
  • To process and record financial contributions and church asset information.
  • To ensure the security and integrity of the App and its data.

4. Data Storage and Security

All personal data is stored securely on Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure, including Azure SQL Database and Azure Blob Storage, located in data centres that comply with international security standards. Access to data is restricted based on your role within the church hierarchy, and all data transmissions are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within OACNZ: Authorised church leadership (Priests, Elders, Overseers, Administrators) may access member data within their assigned scope.
  • Service Providers: We use Microsoft Azure for hosting, and Expo/Firebase for push notification delivery. These providers process data solely to provide their services and are bound by appropriate data protection agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal processes.

6. Push Notifications

The App uses Expo's push notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver notifications to your device. Your device's push token is stored securely and used only to send notifications relevant to your church role and activities. You may disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as you are an active member of OACNZ, or as required by applicable law. If your membership ends, your data may be retained for a reasonable period for record-keeping purposes before being securely deleted or anonymised upon request.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain it.
  • Withdraw consent for push notifications at any time via your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Sunday School records of minors are managed by authorised church administrators only.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
